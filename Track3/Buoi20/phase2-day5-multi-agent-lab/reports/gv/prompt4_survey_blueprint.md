# Prompt 4: Survey Paper Blueprint

### Proposed Paper Title
"AI Agents for Research Assistance: Capabilities, Evaluation, and Open Problems"

### Draft Abstract
The rapid advancement of artificial intelligence (AI) technologies has led to the emergence of AI agents designed to assist researchers in various domains. This survey paper aims to provide a comprehensive overview of the capabilities of AI agents in research assistance, examining their functionalities, effectiveness, and limitations. We categorize existing AI agents based on their roles in the research process, evaluate their performance through established metrics, and identify gaps in current evaluation methodologies. Furthermore, we discuss open problems in the field, including issues of trust, interpretability, and ethical considerations. By highlighting the most pressing challenges and proposing a framework for future benchmarks, this paper aims to guide researchers and practitioners in the development and deployment of AI agents for research assistance.

### Section-by-Section Outline

#### 1. Introduction
- **Purpose**: To introduce the topic of AI agents in research assistance, outlining the significance and relevance of the study.
- **Key Themes**: Definition of AI agents, the evolution of research assistance, and the motivation for this survey.
- **Questions**: What are AI agents? Why are they important for research? What gaps exist in current literature?
- **Likely Pitfalls**: Overgeneralization of AI capabilities, neglecting the diversity of research domains.
- **Open Problems**: Lack of consensus on definitions and classifications of AI agents.

#### 2. Capabilities of AI Agents in Research Assistance
- **Purpose**: To categorize and describe the various capabilities of AI agents that assist researchers.
- **Key Themes**: Information retrieval, data analysis, literature review, experimental design, and writing assistance.
- **Questions**: What specific tasks can AI agents perform? How do these capabilities vary across different domains?
- **Likely Pitfalls**: Failing to provide concrete examples or case studies, leading to a lack of practical understanding.
- **Open Problems**: Integration of multi-modal capabilities (e.g., text, data, and visual analysis) in a single agent.

#### 3. Evaluation Metrics for AI Agents
- **Purpose**: To review existing evaluation metrics used to assess the performance of AI agents in research contexts.
- **Key Themes**: Accuracy, efficiency, user satisfaction, and contextual relevance.
- **Questions**: What metrics are currently employed? How effective are these metrics in capturing the true performance of AI agents?
- **Likely Pitfalls**: Over-reliance on quantitative metrics without considering qualitative aspects.
- **Open Problems**: Development of comprehensive evaluation frameworks that include user-centric metrics.

#### 4. Case Studies of AI Agents in Research
- **Purpose**: To provide detailed case studies of existing AI agents that have been successfully deployed in research settings.
- **Key Themes**: Real-world applications, user feedback, and lessons learned from deployment.
- **Questions**: What are notable examples of AI agents in research? What outcomes have been observed?
- **Likely Pitfalls**: Focusing too narrowly on successful cases and ignoring failures or limitations.
- **Open Problems**: Documentation of long-term impacts of AI agents on research productivity and quality.

#### 5. Challenges and Limitations of AI Agents
- **Purpose**: To discuss the inherent challenges and limitations faced by AI agents in research assistance.
- **Key Themes**: Trustworthiness, interpretability, ethical concerns, and user resistance.
- **Questions**: What are the main challenges in deploying AI agents? How do these challenges affect user adoption?
- **Likely Pitfalls**: Underestimating the complexity of user-agent interactions and the socio-technical landscape.
- **Open Problems**: Strategies for improving user trust and understanding of AI agents.

#### 6. Open Problems in AI Research Assistance
- **Purpose**: To identify and elaborate on specific open problems that need addressing in the field.
- **Key Themes**: Interdisciplinary collaboration, adaptability to diverse research needs, and ethical AI usage.
- **Questions**: What are the most pressing open problems? How can they be prioritized for future research?
- **Likely Pitfalls**: Listing problems without providing actionable insights or research directions.
- **Open Problems**: Development of adaptive AI agents that can learn from user interactions and feedback.

#### 7. Evaluation Gaps in Current Research
- **Purpose**: To critically analyze the existing evaluation frameworks and identify gaps in current research methodologies.
- **Key Themes**: Lack of standardized benchmarks, insufficient longitudinal studies, and limited user-centric evaluations.
- **Questions**: What are the major gaps in evaluation? How do these gaps affect the reliability of research findings?
- **Likely Pitfalls**: Failing to connect gaps to specific research outcomes or user experiences.
- **Open Problems**: Need for collaborative efforts to establish standardized evaluation protocols across different domains.

#### 8. Future Benchmarking for AI Agents
- **Purpose**: To propose a framework for future benchmarks that can effectively measure the performance of AI agents in research assistance.
- **Key Themes**: Comprehensive metrics, user engagement, adaptability, and ethical considerations.
- **Questions**: What should a strong benchmark measure? How can it be implemented across various research domains?
- **Likely Pitfalls**: Creating overly complex benchmarks that are difficult to implement or interpret.
- **Open Problems**: Establishing collaborative benchmarks that incorporate diverse user needs and research contexts.

### Conclusion
This survey aims to provide a structured overview of AI agents for research assistance, highlighting their capabilities, evaluation methods, and the open problems that remain in the field. By addressing these aspects, we hope to contribute to the ongoing discourse and guide future research efforts in developing effective AI agents that can enhance the research process.
